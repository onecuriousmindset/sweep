What leaves your browser

  • The text of a post that comes near your screen, and your rules. They go to the TypeSafe API, with your key.
  • Nothing more. No names, no cookies, no page addresses, no analytics.
  • Your key and your rules stay in this browser. Web pages cannot read them, and they do not sync.
  • A daily limit stops the requests at 5,000 posts. You can change the limit.
